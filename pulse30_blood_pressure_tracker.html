AHA Blood Pressure Categories

American Heart Association guidelines for adult blood pressure classifications:

Normal
Systolic < 120 and Diastolic < 80 mmHg
Elevated
Systolic 120-129 and Diastolic < 80 mmHg
Hypertension Stage 1
Systolic 130-139 or Diastolic 80-89 mmHg
Hypertension Stage 2
Systolic 140 or higher or Diastolic 90 or higher mmHg
Hypertensive Crisis
Systolic higher than 180 and/or Diastolic higher than 120 mmHg (Consult doctor immediately)
